1. Summary

HintFire is a local party game played on a single device. No account is required. The app has no server of its own. Game data is stored on the user's device.

The free version may show advertising supplied by Google AdMob, including an optional rewarded video the user can choose to watch. Active premium access disables interstitial ads; a user with a temporary unlock may choose a rewarded video to extend it. Release builds may connect to Expo Application Services to check for app updates. The App Store and Google Play process payments, and RevenueCat verifies purchase status and premium access. Anonymous usage statistics may be collected through Google Analytics for Firebase, and the user can switch them off in Settings.

Advertising, statistics, purchases and updates are each enabled by configuration. In a build where the corresponding identifiers are not configured, that part of the app is inactive: no ad network is contacted, no statistics are collected, no purchase is made, and no update request is sent.

A short version of the same text is available inside the app under Settings → About → Privacy policy, in Armenian, Russian and English. This page is the full English version.

3. Data stored on the device

The app may store the following data locally:

  • team names, emoji and colors entered by users, and their scores;
  • interface and word language, sound, vibration, accent theme, timer and scoring settings;
  • topics and words the user creates, and saved game setups (presets);
  • the history of words already played, so they are not repeated;
  • results of finished games: teams, scores, dates, per-round statistics;
  • an unfinished game and its current state, so play can resume after the app is closed;
  • the state of premium access: the plan that was bought, or the moment a gifted unlock ends;
  • whether usage statistics are switched on, and the most recent response to the consent form;
  • advertising timings: when the app was first opened, when an ad was last shown, when the rewarded gift or an offer of premium was last shown or taken, how many times the gift was offered and how many extensions of a gift were used;
  • the identifier of the installed app update, so that the notice about it is shown once;
  • a count of finished games and whether a store review has already been requested.

This local game data is used only to provide game features, restore settings and resume play. It is not sent to a server operated by the developer. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information in team names, custom topics or custom words.

The word history and the game statistics can be cleared from the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all app-controlled local data, subject to the device platform's backup and restoration behaviour.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version may show an interstitial advertisement from Google AdMob after a round has been confirmed, and never during a round. The advertisement may be loaded in advance, while the results of the round are on screen. Consecutive interstitials are separated by a minimum interval. Interstitial ads are not requested while premium access is active, whether it was bought or temporarily unlocked. If an ad cannot be loaded, the game continues after the load attempt.

In addition to the interstitial, the app rarely offers an optional rewarded video on the winner screen. It is never shown automatically: it starts only when the user chooses to watch it, and watching it to the end unlocks all premium features for thirty minutes, including the removal of interstitials for that time. Near the end of a gifted unlock the app may offer one more video to extend it, at most twice.

When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location; user interactions with the app or an ad; diagnostic and performance information; and advertising, app-set, device or account-related identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, ad measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. Depending on the user's region, device settings and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized; where consent is required, the choice is collected before advertising is initialized, and the app provides a way to review or change it.

The app does not add team names, scores, played words or custom topics to an advertising request. Android users can reset or delete their advertising ID in device settings. Google's advertising privacy information and controls are available at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and myadcenter.google.com.

5. Usage statistics and Google Analytics

The app may collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. They show how the game is used, so that it can be improved: which screens are opened, which modes and settings are chosen, how long a game lasts, and how the premium screen and the offers are used.

An event may carry:

  • the name of a screen that was opened;
  • the start and the end of a game: the mode, the word language, the number of teams, of built-in topics and of the user's own topics, which of the built-in topics were chosen, the length of a round, the target score, the size of a word card, whether the last-word rule is on, whether the game is a rematch, the number of rounds, the length of the game in minutes and whether it ended with a winner or a draw;
  • purchase steps: opening the premium screen and the screen it was opened from, choosing a plan, and the result of a purchase or purchase restoration;
  • an offer of temporary premium that was shown or accepted, and an advertisement that was shown;
  • the fact that the result of a game or an invitation was shared, without its content or its recipient.

The Firebase SDK adds technical data of its own: a random identifier of the installation, the device model and language, the version of the operating system and of the app, the country derived from the IP address, and the time and length of a session. The IP address is used to derive the country and is not kept in the reports.

Team names, scores, played words, and topics and words made by the user are never part of an event. The statistics are not used for advertising: the analytics SDK is set not to collect the advertising ID, and its data is not shared for the personalization of ads.

Where the law requires consent, statistics are collected only after the user gives consent through the same form used for advertising preferences. In all regions, statistics can be switched off in the app under Settings, About, Usage statistics. This stops further collection of usage statistics.

Google keeps the events for up to fourteen months. Firebase privacy information is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Purchases and RevenueCat

For in-app purchases:

  • the App Store or Google Play processes the payment;
  • RevenueCat retrieves the available products, validates the purchase, restores purchases, and determines whether the premium entitlement is active;
  • RevenueCat may process an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, device type, operating system, purchase history, the store purchase token, and product, transaction and entitlement information;
  • the developer does not receive the user's payment-card details.

Purchase history is used to provide premium access, restore a purchase, prevent entitlement errors and understand purchase performance. The app does not send a custom account ID, name, email address, advertising ID, team names or custom game content to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy information is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. Google explains its data practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and Apple at apple.com/legal/privacy.

7. App updates

Release builds may connect to Expo Application Services to check for and download compatible updates. Network requests necessarily expose ordinary technical information to the service provider, which may include an IP address, app version, platform or device technical information, and request logs. Team names, custom topics and other local game content are not included in the app's update request.

Expo's privacy information is available at expo.dev/privacy.

8. Store review

After several finished games the app may ask the App Store or Google Play to display its own in-app review dialog, and it does so at most once per installation. The store displays and processes the review. The app stores only a local counter and the fact that a review has been requested; it does not receive the review, its rating or its text. Builds without the native review module skip the request silently.

9. Sharing started by the user

Four actions hand data to another application, and only after the user starts them:

  • Share a topic passes the topic's name and words to the app chosen in the system share sheet;
  • Share a game setup passes the preset — team names, colors and rules — the same way;
  • Share the result passes an image of the game results showing team names and scores;
  • Invite friends passes a short fixed invitation text and a link to the app's store page.

The user selects the recipient and the application that receives the message, and that application processes it under its own terms. Played words and statistics are never included. Scores are shared only in the results image.

10. Sharing and sale of data

The developer does not sell personal data.

Technical, advertising, statistical and purchase-related data may be disclosed only to the relevant service providers — Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Expo, the App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat — when needed to provide ads, measurement and fraud prevention, usage statistics, updates, purchases, entitlement validation or restoration. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users, or to protect the app and developer from fraud or abuse.

11. Email contact

When a user chooses to contact the developer from the privacy screen, the app opens the user's email application. The email address, message and any information voluntarily included by the user are processed by the user's email provider and the developer's email provider. Users should not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

12. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. The game contains no chat and no public user-generated content, does not require an account, and does not specifically request a child's personal information. Advertising, when enabled, is not targeted at children. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users, use nicknames instead of full names, and avoid entering personal or sensitive information in custom topics.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child sent personal information to the developer by email, they may contact the developer to request its deletion.

13. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the user clears it in Settings or uninstalls the app. The word history and the statistics have their own reset buttons, and the "Start over" section in Settings deletes all game data at once — the unfinished game, custom topics and words, presets, history, statistics and the settings themselves — without touching purchases. What survives a reset — premium state, advertising timings, the review counter, the identifier of the installed update, the most recent response to the consent form and the choice about usage statistics — is removed by uninstalling the app.

Usage statistics are tied to a random identifier of the installation, not to a name or an account, so the developer cannot tell which events belong to which person. Switching the statistics off stops the collection, uninstalling the app removes the identifier from the device, and Google deletes the events at the end of the retention period.

Purchase records may be retained by the stores and by RevenueCat according to their legal obligations and retention policies. A user may write to armdevstack@gmail.com to request deletion of information controlled through RevenueCat; the developer may need an anonymous RevenueCat App User ID or purchase information sufficient to locate the record. Store transaction records that Apple or Google must retain may not be controlled by the developer.

14. Security

The app minimizes transmitted data and uses the standard security mechanisms provided by the device platform and service providers. Google Mobile Ads, Firebase, RevenueCat and Expo use encrypted network connections for their services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

15. User rights

Depending on their location, users may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of or objection to the processing of personal data connected with the app. Requests can be sent to armdevstack@gmail.com. The developer may request limited information needed to verify and fulfil the request.
