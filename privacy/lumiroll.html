In short

LumiRoll is a single-player platformer for all ages. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your progress and settings stay on your phone.

The game can show ads from Google AdMob: a short video you choose to watch for a reward and, for players aged 13 and over, an occasional full-screen ad at a break between levels. Players under 13 only get child-directed, non-personalised ads. LumiRoll Pro and four characters can be bought once through Google Play; Pro removes all ads. The game also checks Expo for new versions of itself. It has no analytics of its own and works without an internet connection (there are no ads then).

This policy covers version 1.0.1 and later. Version 1.0.0, shared only with testers, had no ads and no purchases.

2.What stays on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, LumiRoll saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • finished levels, collected shards, best times and scores, hearts, sparks and play statistics such as play time, jumps and wins;
  • ghost runs: the button presses of your best runs, used to replay a ghost ball next to you;
  • settings: language, music and sound volume, hints, timer, screen shake, graphics quality, button layout and look;
  • the character and world look you chose;
  • the birth year from the age question, the parental lock setting and which bought items (Pro, characters) your Google account owns.

On the first start the game reads your phone's language list on the device to pick the starting language. Nothing about it leaves the phone.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this app storage in your device backup. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account; the developer has no access to it.

3.The age question

On the first start LumiRoll asks for the year of birth. It does not ask for a name, a date or any other detail. The year is kept only on the device and is used:

  • to switch the parental lock on by default for players under 16, so that a grown-up question appears before any purchase;
  • to choose how ads are requested: for a player under 13 every ad request is marked as directed to a child, and for a player under 16 as under the age of consent, so these players only get non-personalised ads; players under 13 never get the full-screen ads between levels.

Only these marks go with an ad request, never the year itself. A player who skips the question is treated as a child. The year can be removed by clearing the app's data (see section 10).

4.Game updates

When the game starts with an internet connection, it asks Expo Application Services (EAS Update) whether a newer version of the game is available, and downloads it if you choose to install it. Each request contains:

  • a random installation ID, created on the device by the update library; it is not taken from the hardware, the advertising ID or any account, and is replaced when the app is reinstalled;
  • the app version, platform, update channel and the IDs of the installed and running updates;
  • only if an update failed to start: the IDs of up to five failed updates and, once, a short text of the error;
  • your IP address, which any internet request reveals.

Expo uses this, on the developer's behalf, to deliver updates, to count successful and failed installs and to roll updates out gradually. The error text is kept only in raw request logs, which Expo deletes after three days. Expo does not use this information for advertising and does not derive your location from it.

The request contains nothing from your progress, settings or birth year. If there is no connection, the check simply does not happen and the game keeps working. Expo processes this information under its own privacy policy.

5.Google Play and testing

LumiRoll is distributed through Google Play. Installing and updating the app through Google Play is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash and performance reports collected by Android; these do not identify you.

If you join a test of LumiRoll, the email address of your Google account is on a testers list that the developer manages in Google Play Console. It is used only to give you access to the test. Feedback you send through Google Play reaches the developer with the details Google Play attaches to it.

6.Advertising and purchases

Ads. LumiRoll shows ads from Google AdMob, Google's advertising service:

  • a short video, only when you tap to watch one (for a heart, or to try a character or look for one level), at most 10 a day;
  • for players aged 13 and over, a full-screen ad at a natural break after a finished level, at most one every few minutes, never during play and never in the first levels.

Players who own LumiRoll Pro see no ads, and the game does not start the ad service for them.

When an ad is loaded or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game sends Google: your IP address (which can show your approximate location), the advertising ID and app set ID where allowed, information about the device and the app (such as model, Android version, language and app version), how you interact with ads (such as views, taps and finished videos) and diagnostics (such as how long an ad took to load). Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, to limit how often the same ad appears and to detect fraud. How Google uses this information is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are personalised only for adults, and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only after the adult agreed in Google's consent form, shown before the first ad. There, Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game opens the form again to change the choice at any time. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Privacy → Ads). Every ad, for every player, is limited to content rated for families (at most PG; G for players under 13).

Purchases. LumiRoll Pro and the characters Robot, Planet, Dragon and Unicorn can be bought once through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy; the developer never sees card or payment details. Google Play tells the game on your phone which items your Google account owns, and the game keeps that on the device; Restore purchase asks Google Play again, for example after a reinstall. The developer receives Google Play's order records (such as the order number, item, price, country and date), used for accounting, taxes and refunds. When the parental lock is on, a grown-up question appears before Google Play's payment screen.

7.Device permissions

LumiRoll uses only the permissions it needs to run:

  • Internet, for the update check, ads and purchases described above;
  • Vibration, for short taps of feedback during play;
  • Audio settings, to play music and sounds correctly;
  • Google Play billing, to open Google Play's payment screen;
  • Advertising ID, declared by the ad library, so that Google can use the advertising ID for ads where it is allowed (see section 6).

The libraries also declare technical permissions that Android grants without asking: network state (to see whether a connection exists), keeping the device awake while an update downloads, and Android's privacy-preserving advertising features (Ad Services).

The game does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files, and does not request access to them.

8.Children

LumiRoll is made for players of all ages, including children. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information: there is no account, chat or sharing with other players. For a player under 13, or anyone who skipped the age question, every ad request is marked as directed to a child: Google then serves only non-personalised ads rated for all ages, without remarketing, under its rules for children's content. Children never get the full-screen ads between levels, only the videos they choose to watch. Google AdMob takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. A grown-up question appears before any purchase. The update check uses only a random installation ID. The birth year from the age question stays on the device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that information about your child has reached the developer, for example in an email, write to armdevstack@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

9.Sharing and sale of data

The developer does not sell, rent or trade personal information and does not hold any to sell. The game sends data only to:

  • Google, for ads (AdMob, section 6) and for distribution and purchases (Google Play, sections 5 and 6). When an adult agreed to personalised ads, Google may also use the ad data for its own advertising, which some privacy laws call "sharing"; you can refuse or withdraw this as section 6 explains;
  • Expo, which handles the update check as a service provider on the developer's behalf (section 4).

10.Keeping and deleting data

Data on the device stays there until you remove it:

  • Settings → Reset progress erases levels, records, statistics, hearts, sparks and ghost runs;
  • Android Settings → Apps → LumiRoll → Storage → Clear data, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the game saved, including the birth year;
  • your advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android's settings (Privacy → Ads); Google keeps ad data under its own retention rules, described in the Google Privacy Policy;
  • purchases belong to your Google account, not to the phone: clearing data does not remove them, and Restore purchase brings them back.

Because the developer holds no copy of your game data, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. Google Play's order records are kept as long as accounting and tax law require. The installation ID in Expo's update records is not linked to your name or account; reinstalling the app replaces it. Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them and are deleted on request.

11.Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal Android device. Update checks, ads and purchases use encrypted connections, and the app accepts updates only from the developer's own Expo project.

12.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, to correct or delete it, and to object to its processing. Since LumiRoll keeps your game data only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly with the steps in section 10; for ad data, use the choices in section 6 or contact Google. For anything the developer may hold, such as an email you sent, write to the contact below. Requests are answered within 30 days.
