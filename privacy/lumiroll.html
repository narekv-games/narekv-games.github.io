In short

LumiRoll is a single-player platformer for all ages. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your progress and settings stay on your phone.

The current version shows no advertising, sells nothing and contains no analytics or tracking. The only thing it sends over the internet is a check for a new version of the game, answered by Expo, which carries a random installation number and technical details but nothing about you or your play. The game works without an internet connection.

1.Data overview

DataProgress, records, ghost runs, settings, chosen character and world look, the birth year from the age question, Pro state
Where it goesStays on your device. Not sent to the developer or anyone else.
DataA random installation ID, IP address, app version, platform, update channel; after a failed start, the IDs of the failed updates and a short error text
Where it goesSent to Expo Application Services when the game checks for a new version, to deliver updates and count successful and failed installs.
DataYour email address and message
Where it goesOnly if you write to the developer, and only to answer you.

The developer receives no copy of your progress, your settings or your birth year, and has no way to see them.

2.What stays on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, LumiRoll saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • finished levels, collected shards, best times and scores, hearts, sparks and play statistics such as play time, jumps and wins;
  • ghost runs: the button presses of your best runs, used to replay a ghost ball next to you;
  • settings: language, music and sound volume, hints, timer, screen shake, graphics quality, button layout and look;
  • the character and world look you chose;
  • the birth year from the age question, the parental lock setting and whether Pro is active.

On the first start the game reads your phone's language list on the device to pick the starting language. Nothing about it leaves the phone.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this app storage in your device backup. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account; the developer has no access to it.

3.The age question

On the first start LumiRoll asks for the year of birth. It does not ask for a name, a date or any other detail. The year is kept only on the device and is used to switch on the parental lock for younger players, so that a grown-up question appears before anything that a parent should decide.

The year is never sent anywhere and can be removed by clearing the app's data (see section 10).

4.Game updates

When the game starts with an internet connection, it asks Expo Application Services (EAS Update) whether a newer version of the game is available, and downloads it if you choose to install it. Each request contains:

  • a random installation ID, created on the device by the update library; it is not taken from the hardware, the advertising ID or any account, and is replaced when the app is reinstalled;
  • the app version, platform, update channel and the IDs of the installed and running updates;
  • only if an update failed to start: the IDs of up to five failed updates and, once, a short text of the error;
  • your IP address, which any internet request reveals.

Expo uses this, on the developer's behalf, to deliver updates, to count successful and failed installs and to roll updates out gradually. The error text is kept only in raw request logs, which Expo deletes after three days. Expo does not use this information for advertising and does not derive your location from it.

The request contains nothing from your progress, settings or birth year. If there is no connection, the check simply does not happen and the game keeps working. Expo processes this information under its own privacy policy.

5.Google Play and testing

LumiRoll is distributed through Google Play. Installing and updating the app through Google Play is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash and performance reports collected by Android; these do not identify you.

If you join a test of LumiRoll, the email address of your Google account is on a testers list that the developer manages in Google Play Console. It is used only to give you access to the test. Feedback you send through Google Play reaches the developer with the details Google Play attaches to it.

6.Advertising and purchases

The current version contains no advertising network, no in-app purchases and no payment code. While the game is in testing, everything in LumiRoll Pro is open for everyone at no cost.

A later version may offer an optional short video in exchange for a heart and a one-time LumiRoll Pro purchase through Google Play. Before such a version is released, this policy and the game's Data safety information on Google Play will be updated to describe exactly what those services receive. Purchases would always be processed by Google Play; the developer never sees card or payment details.

7.Device permissions

LumiRoll uses only the permissions it needs to run:

  • Internet, for the update check described above;
  • Vibration, for short taps of feedback during play;
  • Audio settings, to play music and sounds correctly.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files, and does not request access to them.

8.Children

LumiRoll is made for players of all ages, including children. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information: there is no account, chat, sharing with other players, advertising or tracking. The update check uses only a random installation ID, never the advertising ID or hardware identifiers. The birth year from the age question stays on the device and is used only to protect younger players.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that information about your child has reached the developer, for example in an email, write to armdevstack@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

9.Sharing and sale of data

The developer does not sell, rent or trade personal information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Expo handles the update check as a service provider on the developer's behalf. Apart from that and distribution through Google Play described above, LumiRoll does not send data to any third party.

10.Keeping and deleting data

Data on the device stays there until you remove it:

  • Settings → Reset progress erases levels, records, statistics, hearts, sparks and ghost runs;
  • Android Settings → Apps → LumiRoll → Storage → Clear data, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the game saved, including the birth year.

Because the developer holds no copy of this data, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. The installation ID in Expo's update records is not linked to your name or account; reinstalling the app replaces it. Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them and are deleted on request.

11.Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal Android device. Update checks and downloads use encrypted HTTPS connections, and the app accepts updates only from the developer's own Expo project.

12.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, to correct or delete it, and to object to its processing. Since LumiRoll keeps your data only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly with the steps in section 10. For anything the developer may hold, such as an email you sent, write to the contact below. Requests are answered within 30 days.

13.Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, for example when advertising or purchases are added, this page will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
